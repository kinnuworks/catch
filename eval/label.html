Label the fine print

For each sentence, pick every kind that applies to the customer. Pick None if it puts no cost, delay or restriction on them. Keys: 1–6 toggle, 0 = None, Enter or → = next, ← = back.

1 Auto-renews
keeps charging until you cancel
2 Trial turns paid
a free or cheap trial becomes a paid plan
3 Lock-in
can't cancel (for free) before a minimum period ends
4 Exit fee
leaving early costs money (a fee, or paying the rest of the term)
5 Cancel rules
needs advance notice, a deadline, or a specific channel (post, phone)
6 No refunds
money already paid isn't returned
